# 🧬 Constitutional Genome

### Agentic Reconstruction & Digital Heritage Intelligence Platform

> **From Documents to Ideas. From Ideas to Constitutional History.**

---

## 📌 Overview

Constitutional Genome is an AI-powered digital heritage intelligence platform designed to preserve, organize, explore and connect historical constitutional documents, writings, manuscripts and debates.

Instead of functioning only as a digital document repository, the platform creates an intelligence layer over archival material by connecting:

**Documents → Evidence → Ideas → Debates → Historical Lineage**

The system is designed for educational institutions, memorials, museums, libraries, researchers and students.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Historical documents, manuscripts, speeches and constitutional debates are often distributed across different archival collections.

Researchers and students may have difficulty:

- Finding relevant information across multiple documents
- Extracting information from scanned documents
- Understanding historical context
- Connecting related constitutional ideas
- Tracing an idea across different historical periods
- Verifying whether an AI-generated interpretation is supported by a source
- Exploring archival information in an interactive way

Constitutional Genome addresses these challenges through an integrated digital archive and AI-assisted research platform.
